Assumptions

  • Horizon: the next {{ plan.horizon }} months, starting next month.
  • Monthly surplus is projected income minus projected expenses. A month with a negative surplus funds nothing and never undoes progress.
  • Safety buffer: {{ plan.buffer_display }}, {% if plan.buffer_source == "household" %}set for your household{% else %}one month of average actual spending over the last 3 complete months{% endif %}. It is set aside once, from the first positive surplus, before any goal is funded.
  • Fill order: priority first (goals without a priority last), a goal never before the goal it depends on, then the order the goals were added. The whole surplus goes to the first unfunded goal before the next.
  • A goal counts as on time when it is fully funded in or before its target month. Current progress comes from the linked account or the manual amount.
